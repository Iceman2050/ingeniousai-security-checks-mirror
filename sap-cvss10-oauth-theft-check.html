What this check does NOT do

This page is an honest exposure triage, not a vulnerability scanner. It cannot scan your systems, cannot determine your installed SDK version, and cannot confirm whether credentials were actually stolen. It scores your exposure and tells you the remediation order — the definitive check is comparing your installed version against SAP's official advisory. And it will not show you a CVE ID: none was published in the verified reports, and we refuse to invent one.

Why OAuth2 theft is the worst-case cybersecurity outcome

  • Tokens are the keys. A stolen OAuth2 token impersonates the application with its full permissions — the attacker doesn't need your password.
  • Patching without rotating is half a fix. If tokens were exfiltrated before you upgraded, the attacker keeps access until every pre-disclosure credential is dead.
  • CVSS 10 means total compromise. The maximum score is reserved for flaws with low attack complexity and full impact on confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
  • Logs are a second crime scene. Tokens embedded in application logs, CI output, or shell history are credential stores too — scrub them as part of the response.
