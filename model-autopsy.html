What was o1 good at?

o1 (Sep 2024 – Oct 2026) was OpenAI's first reasoning model. It worked through problems step by step with an internal chain-of-thought before answering, which made it strong on complex reasoning tasks like math, science and code reasoning — at the cost of slower responses than chat models.

What replaced OpenAI o1?

OpenAI's stated successor path is the GPT-5.x reasoning model line, per OpenAI's own migration guidance. Test the replacement on your own evals before cutting over — behavior, latency and cost will differ from o1.

How do I migrate from a retired AI model?

Audit every place the model is pinned, run the vendor's recommended replacement against your own evals, and compare behavior, latency and cost before switching traffic. Watch pinned model names in config files — they are the most common migration trap.
