What changed on October 1, 2026

Before Oct 1, 2026: free-form replies inside the 24-hour service window (service messages) were free on the WhatsApp Business Platform. Utility templates sent inside an open window were free too.
Since Oct 1, 2026: service messages cost each market's utility rate after 1,000 free per business phone number per month. Utility templates inside the window are charged. Marketing prices reportedly rose in some markets — about +30% reported in Mexico. No payment method on file? Meta stops delivering service messages once the free tier runs out.
Rates vary by country and are yours to enter above. Publicly reported figures (USD): service messages roughly $0.0014 (India), $0.0034 (US), $0.0101 (Nigeria), $0.0040 (most of Africa), $0.0220 (UK); marketing roughly $0.062 (Nigeria), $0.0860 (Malaysia); reported template-rate ranges roughly $0.0109–$0.1597 (marketing) and $0.0008–$0.0550 (utility/auth) per message. The free WhatsApp Business app (replies typed on a phone) is a different product and is not affected.

Sources (October 2026 coverage): dev.to India pricing guide · Sanrovax / Medium · NagMeLater · RakanSales Malaysia · Qontak Indonesia calculator · NewsUltra360 Nigeria. Your Meta rate card is the only official number.

Cut the shock: 4 moves

  • Reclassify templates. Reported figures put marketing at ~7.5x utility (e.g. Rs 0.8631 vs Rs 0.115 in India). Genuine updates filed as marketing templates are the #1 overpayment.
  • Resolve in fewer replies. Every reply past the 1,000 free now has a price. Better first answers and batched deliveries cut the new service-message line directly.
  • Count your numbers. The 1,000 free service messages are per business phone number — businesses running multiple numbers have multiple allowances.
  • Audit marketing lists. With marketing rates reportedly up (+30% reported in Mexico), set-and-forget win-back campaigns may now cost more than they earn.

Frequently asked questions

What changed in WhatsApp Business pricing on October 1, 2026?

Since October 1, 2026, Meta charges WhatsApp Business Platform businesses for service messages — free-form replies inside the 24-hour customer window — at each market's utility rate after 1,000 free per phone number per month. Utility templates sent inside an open service window are now charged too, and marketing prices reportedly rose in some markets (about +30% reported in Mexico).

Is the WhatsApp Business API free for business replies now?

Not anymore. Until September 30, 2026, free-form replies inside the 24-hour service window were free. Since October 1, 2026, the Platform charges for them at the utility rate after 1,000 free per business phone number per month. The free WhatsApp Business app (replies typed on a phone) is a different product and is not affected.

How much does one WhatsApp marketing message cost?

It varies by recipient country. Publicly reported figures include India: marketing Rs 0.8631; Nigeria: marketing about $0.062; Malaysia: marketing $0.0860. The calculator above multiplies YOUR rates by YOUR volumes — every output is an estimate, not Meta's official quote.

What happens if my business has no payment method on Meta's file?

Meta delivers service messages only within the 1,000 free monthly allowance per number, and stops delivering them once the free tier is used up if no valid payment method is on file. The symptom is replies that silently stop partway through a month — check Billing Hub.

What is the WhatsApp Business API cost per message after October 2026?

Meta charges per delivered message by category and recipient country. Publicly reported figures include India: marketing Rs 0.8631, utility/authentication/service Rs 0.115; service-message rates in USD of roughly $0.0014 in India, $0.0034 in the US, $0.0040 across most of Africa and $0.0220 in the UK. All figures are estimates based on publicly reported rate cards, not Meta's official quote.

How can a business lower its WhatsApp bill after the price increase?

Reclassify templates — marketing costs roughly 7–8x utility, so genuine updates filed as marketing templates are the #1 overpayment — resolve cases in fewer replies with better first answers, remember the 1,000 free service messages are per phone number, and audit marketing lists now that rates have risen.
