Spot the AI Video Duel

Two clips. One is AI-generated. One is real camera footage. You get about 10 seconds to decide — then instant right-or-wrong, a final verdict, and a shareable score card.

Honesty first: Rounds 1–2 are true duels — an AI-generated clip vs real, freely-licensed camera footage (source + license shown under each clip after you guess). Rounds 3–4 are practice rounds where both clips are AI-generated, clearly labeled, and they don't count toward your score. No clip is ever presented as real footage when it isn't.

How to play

  • Watch both clips (they loop, sound off).
  • Tap "This one is AI" under the clip you think was generated by AI.
  • Only the 2 true-duel rounds count toward your score.
  • Share your verdict card and challenge a friend.

For fun and education — spotting tells like warping backgrounds, too-smooth motion, or physics that feels "off". Not a forensic detection tool.

    What to watch for

    • Backgrounds that breathe: walls, fences or water that warp when the subject moves.
    • Too-perfect motion: unnaturally smooth, floaty movement with no camera shake.
    • Physics slips: steam, shadows or reflections that don't behave like the real world.
    • Texture tells: skin, fur or fabric that looks painted rather than photographed.

    None of these are proof — they're tells. Real footage can look strange, and AI keeps getting better. That's the game.

    Drop your clips here (for the site owner)

    This game reads its rounds from videos/duel/rounds.json. To add your own AI vs real rounds (e.g. clips from Veo 3.1, Kling or Seedance):

    How to add a round
    1. Export your clip as MP4, H.264, 640px wide, under 2 MB. One ffmpeg line that does it:
      ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -vf scale=640:-2 -c:v libx264 -crf 28 -preset fast -an -movflags +faststart -t 10 videos/duel/my-clip.mp4
    2. Put the file in videos/duel/ next to the existing ai-*.mp4 clips.
    3. Edit videos/duel/rounds.json — each entry is:
      {"subject":"…","ai":"videos/duel/ai-x.mp4","real":"videos/duel/real-x.mp4","ai_credit":"…","real_credit":"…","real_page":"https://…","practice":false}
    4. Honesty rule: ai_credit must say the clip is AI-generated; real_credit must name the real footage source + license. Never mark an AI clip as real.
    5. To keep a placeholder slot: set "practice": true and note "real_note" — the round is then clearly labeled "practice" and unscored.
    6. Commit and push — the game picks up the new rounds.json on the next page load.
