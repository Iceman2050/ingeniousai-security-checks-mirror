PRIVACY INCIDENT

Meta Smart Glasses Recorded Audio Without the Wake Phrase

What happened, what Meta said — and the parts it didn't say.

What happened

  • ~Oct 13, 2026 — Reddit users reported finding ordinary, everyday conversations saved in the Meta AI app: audio recordings plus text transcripts — on glasses where the assistant had never been intentionally activated.
  • Oct 13, 2026 — One owner's report (per Techtimes UK): the "Respond without Hey Meta" setting was disabled, the behavior spanned three separate pairs (2nd- and 3rd-generation Ray-Ban Meta glasses), and the glasses' LED notification light appeared to activate during normal conversation. The user first noticed it after a recent firmware update — there is no evidence the update caused it.
  • Oct 13, 2026 — Meta told Android Authority the issue "has been resolved". It named no cause, no firmware version, and no count of affected owners.
  • Oct 16, 2026 — PCMag surfaced the story: Meta admits its smart glasses briefly recorded audio without a wake phrase, and has not explained what caused the issue.

Honest scope: Android Authority reported the complaints remained limited, were not independently reproduced, and there is no evidence the problem was widespread. Treat this as a real but limited incident — not a confirmed mass surveillance event.

What was recorded

  • Audio recordings and written transcripts of conversations the wearers say they never asked Meta AI to capture.
  • They appeared in the Meta AI app's activity history — visible to the glasses' owner, but recorded from conversations with people around them who never consented.
  • Security researchers at IPVM examined related cases where the LED indicator either failed to light consistently or recording began before the wearer finished the wake phrase — meaning audio could be captured before the visible warning caught up.

What Meta said

  • "This issue has been resolved."
  • Users can "manage, review and delete voice logs of their interactions with Meta AI via settings at any time."

What Meta didn't say

  • What caused the bug.
  • Which firmware version introduced or fixed it.
  • How many owners were affected.
  • What happens to audio captured through an unintended activation (Android Authority asked; the published statement didn't address it).

The US vs UK privacy notice difference

Reporting on Meta's AI Glasses Voice Privacy Notice found the US and UK versions differ:

  • US: Meta will "store voice recordings even if you unintentionally activate a voice interaction."
  • UK: with storage off, recordings are deleted "immediately after processing."

Check the version that applies in your region — the protections are not the same everywhere.

The bystander problem

The LED indicator is the entire social contract that makes camera-and-microphone glasses tolerable around other people: you can at least see when you are being recorded. This incident broke that contract twice over — recordings happened without the wake phrase, and the indicator reportedly didn't light reliably. For the people around a wearer, who never agreed to be recorded, that's the serious part.

Background: the glasses were already restricted in some New York and UK courtrooms over filming-without-consent concerns, and Norway is considering stricter measures on their use in public spaces.

What to do now

  1. Check your Meta AI activity log for recordings you didn't trigger. If anything looks unintended, note the date.
  2. Review and delete voice logs in the Meta AI app settings — audio and transcripts.
  3. Turn OFF "Respond without Hey Meta" if it's on — this feature lets the assistant stay active on natural conversation without the full wake phrase.
  4. Keep firmware updated — Meta deployed the fix through a software update.
  5. Tell people around you what the LED indicator means — and that it hasn't always worked reliably.

FAQ

Did Meta confirm the glasses recorded audio without the wake phrase?

Yes. Meta told Android Authority on October 13, 2026 that "this issue has been resolved" after Reddit users reported audio recordings and transcripts appearing in the Meta AI app without intentional activation. Meta did not explain the cause.

Was the "Respond without Hey Meta" setting the cause?

Not according to the reports — the most detailed Reddit report said the setting was disabled when the unintended recordings occurred. The actual cause remains unknown; Meta named none.

How do I check for unintended recordings?

Open the Meta AI app, go to settings, and review your activity/conversation history for audio recordings and transcripts you didn't ask for. You can review and delete voice logs there at any time.

Does the US privacy notice really differ from the UK one?

Per press reporting, yes: the US version of Meta's AI Glasses Voice Privacy Notice says voice recordings are stored even after unintentional activation, while the UK version says recordings are deleted immediately after processing when storage is off. Check the notice for your region.

Was this a widespread problem?

Android Authority reported the complaints remained limited, were not independently reproduced, and there is no evidence it was widespread. Meta did not publish a count of affected owners.
