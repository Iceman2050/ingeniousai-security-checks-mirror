Frequently asked questions

What is AI vendor lock-in?

When switching AI providers becomes expensive or risky because your workflows, data, or integrations depend on one vendor's proprietary features.

How do you calculate AI switching costs?

Score how deeply each tool is embedded — data gravity, custom integrations, retraining needs — then estimate migration effort. This calculator gives an illustrative 0–100 score with the math shown.

How can I reduce vendor lock-in?

Prefer open standards and portable data formats, keep prompts and evals vendor-neutral, and document exit paths before you need them.
