How do I know if someone is distilling my AI model?

Look for the pattern OpenAI disrupted: high-volume, highly-correlated queries from few API keys, systematic reasoning-heavy prompts and paraphrase chains, bursts of new scraping-style accounts — sustained over time. One anomaly is noise; several together are a campaign. Preserve your query logs, rate-limit the suspicious keys, strip chain-of-thought from outputs, and escalate with evidence.

What is model distillation?

Model distillation is a technique for training a smaller AI model to imitate a larger one by learning from its outputs. It is legitimate research — until it is used to siphon a competitor's protected reasoning at scale, which is a cybersecurity problem OpenAI disrupted in October 2026.

Response steps if signals are strong

  • Preserve logs first. Export query logs for the suspicious keys and time window before retention ages them out.
  • Rate-limit and quota-cap by behavior, not just by key — distillation moves when you ban a key.
  • Strip chain-of-thought reasoning from API outputs; serve final answers only.
  • Require justification for bulk or burst access; gate high-rate tiers behind review.
  • Escalate to your abuse and legal teams with the log evidence — and consider watermarking outputs to trace leaks.

Cybersecurity FAQ

What was the model distillation attack OpenAI disrupted?

OpenAI disrupted a coordinated distillation campaign designed to extract protected reasoning from its AI models. The core cluster of activity was attributed to individuals associated with Moonshot AI, and the campaign originated in early July 2026.

What does a model distillation attack look like in API logs?

High-volume, highly correlated queries from a small set of API keys; systematic edge-case and reasoning-heavy prompts; bursts of new accounts with scraping-style usage; and paraphrase chains that map a model's behavior. Each is a signal, not proof.

Does a single anomaly mean I'm being distilled?

No. Individual signals have benign explanations — a power user, a benchmark, a load test. Distillation is a pattern: multiple signals together, from the same keys or accounts, sustained over time.

What should I do if I suspect distillation?

Preserve query logs first, then rate-limit and quota-cap the suspicious keys by behavior, require justification for bulk access, strip or watermark chain-of-thought outputs, and escalate to your abuse and legal teams with the log evidence.
