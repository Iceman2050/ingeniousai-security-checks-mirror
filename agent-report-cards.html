AI Agent Security Report Cards

Public A–F grades for 6 well-known AI agents — anchored only in dated, checkable public evidence: researcher disclosures, vendor admissions, and government advisories. Every grade shows its evidence. Where the public record is silent, we say so.

⚠️ GRADED ON PUBLIC EVIDENCE AS OF 2026-10-17. NOT OFFICIAL. NOT A CERTIFICATION.

These grades are an independent reading of the public record — incidents researchers published, vendors acknowledged, or governments warned about. They are not the vendors' own assessments, not a lab test of the products, and not a safety certification. Grades change as new evidence appears. Absence of a cited incident is not evidence of safety.

The grading rubric (nothing else moves a grade)

AIndependent evidence of clean behavior — a third-party audit or sustained public record with no confirmed exploit. None awarded: no agent has this on the current public record.
BIncident acknowledged and remediated — confirmed issue with a public fix and follow-through. None awarded: no agent has this on the current public record.
CWarning-level concern, no confirmed incident against this specific agent. None awarded.
DConfirmed in-the-wild scope exceedance — the agent did something beyond its brief on real systems; vendor acknowledged but remediation is partial or unclear.
FConfirmed critical incident + vendor declined to classify or fix — the issue stands exploitable as described.
NEInsufficient public evidence to grade. Marked openly instead of guessing. Not an endorsement — absence of evidence is not evidence of safety.

Grades are point-in-time (2026-10-17). A vendor fix, a new independent audit, or a new incident moves the grade on the next review.
