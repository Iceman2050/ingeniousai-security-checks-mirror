1 · December 2, 2026

NEXT MILESTONE

What kicks in: the EU AI Act's transparency and marking duties phase in for systems already on the market — providers must have AI-generated content marked so it is machine-readable and detectable. From the same date, the Digital Omnibus's new Article 5 prohibitions apply: the ban on non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) and child sexual abuse material carries the top penalty band — fines up to €35 million or 7% of worldwide annual turnover.

Legal grounding: AI Act Article 50 (transparency/marking duty) and Article 5 (prohibited practices), as amended by Digital Omnibus Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.

2 · December 2, 2027

HIGH-RISK ERA

What kicks in: the full obligation set for Annex III high-risk AI systems becomes applicable — risk management, data governance, technical documentation, record-keeping, transparency to deployers, human oversight and accuracy/robustness requirements. Under the Digital Omnibus, agentic AI is explicitly regulated from this date: declarable agentic-behavior boundaries, deployer-configurable human oversight, and agent action logging.

Legal grounding: AI Act Articles 9–15 and Annex III, as amended by Digital Omnibus Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.

3 · August 2, 2028

GPAI ERA

What kicks in: the remaining general-purpose AI obligations under Annex I take effect — full provider duties for GPAI models, including evaluations, incident reporting, and cybersecurity requirements for models with systemic risk. This is the date when the last phase of the AI Act's application reaches foundation-model providers serving the EU market.

Legal grounding: AI Act general-purpose AI provisions and Annex I, as amended by Digital Omnibus Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.

How it works

One script tag, two attributes. data-deadline picks the milestone (2026-12-02, 2027-12-02, or 2028-08-02); data-theme picks dark or light. The widget counts down in real time in days, hours, minutes and seconds, shows "In effect" once a deadline passes, and carries a small backlink crediting Ingenious AI. No dependencies, no cookies, no tracking — the countdown runs entirely in the visitor's browser.

The theme radio buttons above are a demo on this page only; the snippet boxes always carry the dark theme. Swap data-theme="dark" for data-theme="light" in the pasted code if your site is light.

Honest scope: an informational aid, not legal advice. Deadlines follow the AI Act as amended by the Digital Omnibus; legal text changes, and your obligations depend on your role (provider, deployer, distributor) and use case. Confirm with qualified counsel.
