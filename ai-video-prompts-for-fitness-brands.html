What are AI video prompts for fitness brands?

AI video prompts for fitness brands are written scene descriptions that a text-to-video tool turns into short gym clips. A good prompt fixes the action, camera movement, lighting, on-screen text, audio, duration and aspect ratio in advance, so a small team can produce consistent workout, class and equipment videos without a camera crew.

You paste a finished specification instead of a vague idea: who is in frame, what happens second by second, how the clip ends. Replace the bracketed placeholders with your gym or class name, and the same prompt makes a fresh clip for every offer.
