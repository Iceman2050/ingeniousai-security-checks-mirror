OBITUARY

o1 2024 – 2026

"It thought before it spoke — and billed you for the thinking."

Born September 12, 2024, as o1-preview and o1-mini — OpenAI's first "reasoning" model series, formerly known inside the lab as Strawberry. Died October 23, 2026, retired in OpenAI's shutdown wave (gpt-4-0613, gpt-4-turbo, o3-mini, o4-mini, gpt-image-1 and ft-* models went with it).

o1 was the model that made chain-of-thought a product: reinforcement learning taught it to refine its own reasoning, try different strategies, and catch its own mistakes before answering. It scored 83% on the International Mathematics Olympiad qualifying exam (GPT-4o: 13%) and ranked among the top 500 in an American high-school math competition.

It was slower than GPT-4o, worse at images and function calling, and it invented the "reasoning token" — compute you pay for but never see. Developers loved it for math, science and code; finance teams learned to fear the invoice.

What it was good at

  • Complex multi-step reasoning: math, science, competitive programming
  • Self-correction: fewer hallucinations on reasoning-heavy tasks than its peers
  • Starting the reasoning-model era — every "thinking" model since is its descendant
