o1 RETIRED OCT 23, 2026

Born: September 12, 2024 (o1-preview / o1-mini) — OpenAI's first reasoning model, the one that learned to "think before it answers." Died: October 23, 2026, in OpenAI's shutdown wave alongside gpt-4-0613, gpt-4-turbo, o3-mini, o4-mini, gpt-image-1 and ft-* models.

It scored 83% on the International Mathematics Olympiad qualifying exam where GPT-4o managed 13%. It will be remembered for chain-of-thought reasoning and for making "thinking tokens" a line item on everyone's bill.
