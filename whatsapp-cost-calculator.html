What is WhatsApp Business per-message pricing?

WhatsApp Business per-message pricing is the billing model Meta introduced on October 1, 2026: instead of paying per 24-hour conversation, a business pays for each delivered template or service message, at a rate set by the recipient's country and the message category — marketing, utility, authentication, or service.

Two facts drive most new bills. Meta moved WhatsApp Business to per-message pricing on October 1, 2026, ending the era when service replies inside the customer window were free. The first 1,000 service messages per phone number each month stay free; billing starts at the 1,001st message. In Germany, for example, a marketing template costs $0.1365 while a service reply costs $0.0550 — the category a template is filed under matters as much as the volume.

Frequently asked questions

How much does WhatsApp Business cost per message?

It depends on the recipient's market and the message category. In North America a service reply costs $0.0034 and a marketing template costs $0.0250 per delivered message, while in Germany the same messages cost $0.0550 and $0.1365. The calculator above applies Meta's October 1, 2026 rate card for 13 markets.

What changed on October 1, 2026?

Meta replaced per-conversation billing with per-message billing. Service replies and utility messages sent inside the customer window, which used to be free, are now charged per delivered message once a number passes its 1,000 free monthly service messages. Incoming customer messages remain free, and rates can change quarterly.

How can a business cut its WhatsApp bill?

Three levers cut most bills: consolidate multi-message replies into one message, reclassify templates that were flagged as marketing when they are really utility, and route re-engagement through the free 72-hour click-to-WhatsApp window instead of paid marketing templates.
