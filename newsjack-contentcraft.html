The 5-part AI content brief (copy-paste)

1. AUDIENCE (who is this for, specifically?) "Write for [e.g. first-time Shopify store owners doing under $10k/mo]. They are skeptical of gurus and short on time." 2. VOICE (how should it sound?) "Voice: direct, plain-spoken, zero hype. Short sentences. Never use: 'delve', 'tapestry', 'game-changer', 'in today's fast-paced world'. If a sentence could be on anyone's blog, rewrite it." 3. STRUCTURE (make it skimmable + SEO-shaped) "Structure: hook in the first 2 lines, then H2 sections answering one question each. End each section with a one-line takeaway. Total: 800-1200 words." 4. FACTS (anchor it to reality) "Only include claims I can verify. Where I give you a fact below, use it; where I don't, write around it — never invent statistics, quotes, or case studies. Facts: [paste 2-3 real ones here]." 5. CTA (one job for the reader) "End with exactly one call to action: [e.g. 'reply with your biggest bottleneck']. No 'in conclusion'. No summary paragraph."

Make every piece this good, systematically

  • Save the brief as a template — one house brief, reused and refined, beats reinventing it per post.
  • Keep a facts file — verified claims, numbers, and quotes your content can draw on without inventing.
  • Edit like an enemy — cut every sentence that could appear on a competitor's blog.
  • Repurpose deliberately — one strong piece becomes the thread, the newsletter, and the video script.
