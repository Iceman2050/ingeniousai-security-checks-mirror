What is the weakest stage of a SaaS go-to-market?

The weakest stage is whichever of the five go-to-market stages — ICP and market research, content and distribution, demand generation, sales and conversion, retention and expansion — scores lowest on a structured audit. In most early SaaS teams it is distribution or demand generation: the product exists, but no repeatable channel exists, so pipeline depends on luck instead of motion.

FAQ

What are the 5 stages of a SaaS go-to-market?

ICP and market research (who you sell to), content and distribution (how they hear of you), demand generation (how leads arrive), sales and conversion (how leads become customers), and retention and expansion (how customers stay and grow).

Can you describe your beachhead segment in one sentence?

If you can't name your beachhead — the one segment you can win first — in a single sentence without saying “everyone,” your targeting is too broad. Broad targeting means targeting nobody, and it is the most common GTM killer. Write the one-sentence ICP and pin it where your whole team sees it. (Per Prospeo's 2026 go-to-market diagnostic: https://prospeo.io/s/go-to-market-strategy-questions)

Have sales, marketing, and product agreed on the same ICP?

If each team runs its own version of the strategy — three ICPs, three messages, three success metrics — you get fragmented effort and zero pipeline. Alignment on one ICP, one core message, and shared success metrics is a pre-launch diagnostic every SaaS team should run. (Per Prospeo's 2026 go-to-market diagnostic: https://prospeo.io/s/go-to-market-strategy-questions)

How often should a SaaS team re-run the diagnostic?

Quarterly is a good cadence: fix the weakest stage, then re-run to confirm the score moved and find the next bottleneck.
