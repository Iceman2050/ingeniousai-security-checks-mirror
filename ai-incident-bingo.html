What are good cybersecurity quiz questions?

Good cybersecurity quiz questions test judgment against real incidents — like the 24 on this card: prompt injection, data poisoning, rogue agent actions. Scenario-based questions beat trivia because they mirror what teams actually face.

How do I train employees on cybersecurity?

Keep it short, repeatable, and relevant: play this bingo card with your team monthly, walk through each marked incident, and pair it with a phishing quiz. Fifteen minutes of real examples beats an annual slide deck.
