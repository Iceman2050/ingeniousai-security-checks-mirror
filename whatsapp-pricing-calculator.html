FAQ

What is the WhatsApp Business API cost per message after October 2026?

Meta charges per delivered message by category and recipient country. Publicly reported figures include India: marketing Rs 0.8631, utility/authentication/service Rs 0.115; service-message rates in USD of roughly $0.0014 in India, $0.0034 in the US, $0.0040 across most of Africa and $0.0220 in the UK. All figures are estimates based on publicly reported rate cards, not Meta's official quote.

What changed in WhatsApp Business pricing on October 1, 2026?

Three things: (1) service messages — replies inside the 24-hour customer window — are now charged at the utility rate after 1,000 free per number/month; (2) utility templates sent inside an open service window, previously free, are now charged; (3) marketing message prices rose in some markets — about +30% has been reported for Mexico.

How can a business lower its WhatsApp bill after the price increase?

The biggest lever is message classification: marketing templates cost roughly 7-8x utility templates, so reclassifying genuine updates as utility templates cuts bills fast. Then reduce reply volume per case, keep the 1,000 free service messages per number in mind, and audit which conversations actually need to be marketing messages.
