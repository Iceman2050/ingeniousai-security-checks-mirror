What changed on October 1, 2026

Before Oct 1, 2026: free-form replies inside the 24-hour service window (service messages) were free. Utility templates sent inside an open window were free too.
Since Oct 1, 2026: service messages cost each market's utility rate after 1,000 free per business phone number per month. Utility templates inside the window are charged. Marketing prices rose in some markets — about +30% reported in Mexico. No payment method on file? Meta stops delivering service messages once the free tier runs out.
Rates vary by country. Publicly reported figures: service messages roughly $0.0014 (India), $0.0034 (US), $0.0040 (most of Africa), $0.0220 (UK); template rates range roughly $0.0109–$0.1597 (marketing) and $0.0008–$0.0550 (utility/auth) per message.

Rate figures above come from publicly reported Meta rate cards and third-party breakdowns (dev.to pricing guides, Wamafy, Business Standard, NagMeLater, October 2026). This page's calculator converts them into bill estimates — your actual Meta invoice is the only official number.

Cut the new bill: 4 moves

  • Reclassify templates. A marketing message costs ~7.5x a utility message (e.g. Rs 0.8631 vs Rs 0.115 in India). Genuine updates filed as marketing templates are the #1 overpayment.
  • Resolve in fewer replies. Every reply now has a price after the 1,000 free. Better first answers and batched deliveries cut the service-message line directly.
  • Count your numbers. The 1,000 free service messages are per business phone number — businesses running multiple numbers have multiple allowances.
  • Audit marketing send lists. With marketing rates up (reported +30% in Mexico), win-back campaigns you "set and forgot" may now cost more than they earn.

Frequently asked questions

What is the WhatsApp Business API cost per message after October 2026?

Meta charges per delivered message by category and recipient country. Publicly reported figures include India: marketing Rs 0.8631, utility/authentication/service Rs 0.115; service-message rates in USD of roughly $0.0014 in India, $0.0034 in the US, $0.0040 across most of Africa and $0.0220 in the UK. The calculator above turns these into bill estimates — all figures are estimates, not Meta's official quote.

Is WhatsApp Business API free for business replies?

Not anymore. Until September 30, 2026, free-form replies inside the 24-hour service window (service messages) were free. Since October 1, 2026, Meta charges for them at each market's utility rate after 1,000 free service messages per business phone number per month. The free WhatsApp Business app (replies typed on a phone) is a different product and is not affected.

What changed in WhatsApp Business pricing on October 1, 2026?

Three things: (1) service messages — replies inside the 24-hour customer window — are now charged at the utility rate after 1,000 free per number/month; (2) utility templates sent inside an open service window, previously free, are now charged; (3) marketing message prices rose in some markets — about +30% has been reported for Mexico.

How can a business lower its WhatsApp bill after the price increase?

Reclassify templates (marketing costs ~7.5x utility), resolve cases in fewer replies, spread volume across phone numbers (1,000 free service messages each), and audit marketing lists now that marketing rates have risen.
