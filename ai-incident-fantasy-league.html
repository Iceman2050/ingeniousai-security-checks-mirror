Frequently asked questions

What is the AI Incident Fantasy League?

A fantasy game where you draft teams of AI vendors and score points when they suffer publicly-reported incidents, breaches, and outages.

How do you score AI incidents?

Each publicly-reported incident earns points by severity class — all incidents are cited with their source.

What counts as an AI incident?

Breaches, outages, safety failures, and major policy reversals at AI companies — anything the public record documents.
