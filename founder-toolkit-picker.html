How to use this list without burning cash:
• Start free: Google Trends, Google Forms, Reddit, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, BetaList.
• Validate before you pay: run one survey + one community post before buying any subscription.
• Pay only for revenue work: SEMrush, Ahrefs, Statista, Crunchbase and PitchBook are professional tools — buy them when the channel pays for itself.
• Recheck pricing: freemium limits and plan prices change often; the one-liners above describe what each tool does, not what it costs.
