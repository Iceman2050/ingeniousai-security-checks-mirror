What the index reveals

Labeling is winning; training-data honesty is losing. Nearly every vendor now labels AI-generated content somehow (C2PA, SynthID, or platform labels) — the EU AI Act's Article 50 made that unavoidable. But only Adobe publishes a clear, specific account of what its models trained on. Training-data opacity remains the industry's weakest disclosure practice.

The "Unverified" column is a feature, not a gap: vendors that publish their practices get credit the same day we can cite them. Vendors: send us your disclosure pages to claim your points.
