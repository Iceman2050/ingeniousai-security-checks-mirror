The deadlines

General-purpose AI model obligations

Applied 2 August 2025
● IN FORCE NOW
  • Providers of general-purpose AI models (foundation models) already had to put documentation, evaluation, and incident-reporting in place.
  • If you build on top of a foundation model — you're a deployer, not a provider — but check your vendor's compliance posture: their gaps can become your liability.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act), application timeline — official legal text on EUR-Lex.

Frequently asked questions

What are the EU AI Act enforcement deadlines?

Article 50 transparency obligations apply now (machine-readable marking deadline 2 Dec 2026 for pre-existing systems); high-risk system duties apply from 2 Dec 2027; the Act fully applies from 2 Aug 2028.

When does EU AI Act Article 50 apply?

Deployer disclosure duties have applied since 2 August 2026; provider machine-readable marking duties phase in by 2 December 2026.

What happens if I miss an AI compliance deadline?

National regulators can investigate and fine. The ticker above tracks each verified deadline live so teams can plan ahead.
