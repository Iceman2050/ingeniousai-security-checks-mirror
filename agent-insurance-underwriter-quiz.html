Frequently asked questions

What does AI agent liability insurance cover?

Emerging AI agent products cover professional-liability-style losses: an agent makes a mistake a human would have been covered for under your errors-and-omissions policy — wrong data in a client deliverable, a misquoted scope, confidential data surfaced to the wrong party. They generally do not cover a rogue agent causing physical harm. Coverage terms vary by carrier and policy, so read the actual wording.

Why are traditional insurers retreating from AI risk?

Reported in January 2026: Armilla AI raised Lloyd's-backed AI coverage to $25 million as traditional carriers narrowed their AI coverage. Carriers are still pricing a new, fast-moving risk with limited loss history — so specialist capacity is growing while some traditional lines pull back.

What are the ISO AI exclusion forms?

Verisk/ISO made AI-related exclusion forms — CG 40 47, CG 40 48, and CG 35 08 — available effective January 1, 2026. Carriers adopt them individually, so a general-liability policy may now exclude AI-related losses unless endorsed back in. Ask your broker to confirm per line, in writing.

Do I need insurance if I only use AI tools rather than build agents?

Insurers are starting to ask about any business AI use — processing customer data with AI, training data practices, vendor due diligence, and human oversight of AI output. Even off-the-shelf tools can raise questions about data handling and output review. This quiz's data-handling and governance sections apply whether you build agents or buy them.

Will this quiz get me an insurance quote?

No. This is a readiness self-check, not a quote, an application, or a promise of coverage. There is no standard question set — every carrier writes its own application and AI supplement. Use your score to fix the gaps, then talk to a broker who places AI and technology E&O risk.
