What is AI governance, and what does this suite cover?

AI governance is the practice of controlling how AI systems are used: what they may disclose, which models and vendors you depend on, what agents may do, and what records prove it. This suite covers that ground with 21 live Ingenious AI platforms, one per failure point, each free to start.
