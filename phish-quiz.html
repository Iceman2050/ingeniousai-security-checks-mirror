How the quiz works

Each scenario is built from a documented, real-world attack pattern — spear-phishing, lookalike domains, QR-code phishing ("quishing"), MFA fatigue, SMS package scams, AI-cloned voice calls, job-offer scams, and the fake-CAPTCHA malware trick. FAQ

Are these real phishing messages?

They are realistic reconstructions of documented attack patterns, not copies of any single real message. Every scenario reflects techniques security teams have actually observed in the wild — the domains and names are fictional.

Why are two scenarios legitimate?

Because in real life, most messages are fine — and treating everything as hostile trains the wrong reflex. The skill is telling the difference, which is what the quiz scores.

Does the quiz collect my answers?

No. Everything runs in your browser. No account, no tracking of your score, nothing sent anywhere.

I got phished by one of these in real life. What now?

Act fast: change the password from a clean device, revoke sessions, enable two-factor authentication, and if money moved, call your bank immediately. Then report it — to your IT team, or to your country's phishing-report address.
