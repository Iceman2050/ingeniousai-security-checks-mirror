FAQ

Why track this?

Vendor changes break products silently: a deprecated model, a doubled price, a new default-off control. Teams learn about them from their users. This tracker surfaces them with dates and sources.

How do I get alerted automatically?

TermsWatch monitors your AI vendors' terms continuously and alerts you when clauses change — free tier available.

Can I suggest a change to track?

Yes — send the vendor, the change, and a source link via the TermsWatch contact page.

Does ChatGPT train on my data?

It depends on the vendor's current terms and your plan's settings — and those terms change. This page tracks dated, sourced changes to AI vendors' terms so you can see when training clauses move. Always verify against the vendor's current policy.

How do I stop AI companies training on my data?

Check your account's data-controls or opt-out settings, prefer business or enterprise tiers that contractually exclude training, and avoid pasting sensitive content into consumer chatbots. Then watch the terms — this tracker logs every dated change.

What should I check in an AI vendor's terms of service?

Training and data-use rights, retention periods, deletion on request, liability and indemnity clauses, subprocessor lists, and how the vendor may change the terms. Our sister check walks you through the five clauses to Ctrl+F.
