Shadow AI FAQ

What is shadow AI?

Shadow AI is employees using AI tools — personal chatbots, coding assistants, voice notetakers — for work without company approval or oversight. Company data flows into accounts and terms of service the organization never reviewed.

What is the difference between shadow AI and shadow IT?

Shadow IT is the older problem: unapproved software and devices on the network. Shadow AI is its AI-era form — unsanctioned AI tools that ingest your data, where the risk is not just access but that prompts may train models or leak through consumer accounts outside corporate control.

What are the risks of shadow AI?

Customer data, source code, and financials can end up in personal AI accounts outside corporate control, with no audit trail and no incident-response path. Industry surveys in 2026 found large shares of workers pasting credentials or API keys into AI tools and ignoring workplace AI-use restrictions.
