Is your AI-generated ad EU AI Act disclosure-compliant?

The EU AI Act requires providers to disclose when content is AI-generated, and ad platforms are tightening their own labeling rules. Fines run up to €15M or 3% of global turnover. The 5-point self-check

1

Is the label visible at first glance? Open your ad and look for the AI-disclosure label within the first second. If a viewer has to hunt for it, it doesn't count.

Fail: label buried in a caption, description, or behind a "more" click.
Pass: label visible on the creative itself, instantly.
2

Does it survive cropping and re-upload? Platforms crop, compress, and re-encode. If your disclosure lives only in metadata or a caption, it dies on re-upload.

Fail: disclosure only in file metadata, alt text, or post caption.
Pass: disclosure burned into the visual or audio of the creative.
3

Is it in the viewer's language? "AI-generated" means nothing to a Spanish-speaking audience. Check every market you run in.

Fail: English-only label on ads served to non-English audiences.
Pass: localized labels (e.g. EN/ES) per market.
4

Does it survive the publishing pipeline? Pull your ad back down from the platform and check: is the label still there after their processing?

Fail: you never verified what the published version looks like.
Pass: you spot-checked the live, platform-processed creative.
5

Is it documented? If a regulator asks tomorrow, can you show which creatives are AI-generated, when they were labeled, and with what wording?

Fail: no record — it's someone's memory.
Pass: a dated log of creatives, labels, and versions.

If you failed any check, do this now

  • Add on-creative labels to every AI-generated ad — visible, localized, and baked into the media itself.
  • Re-verify after publishing — download the live ad from each platform and confirm the label survived.
  • Start a disclosure log — creative name, date, label text, language. A spreadsheet today beats a fine tomorrow.
  • Automate it — manual checks don't scale past a handful of creatives.

FAQ — EU AI Act disclosure

Does AI-generated content have to be labelled under the EU AI Act?

Yes, in most cases. Deployers of AI-generated image, audio or video must disclose it as AI-generated — the Article 50(4) deployer disclosure duty has applied since 2 August 2026 — and providers must ensure AI outputs are marked so they are machine-readable and detectable.

What are the labelling requirements for AI-generated content?

The notice must be clear and distinguishable, and given at first exposure. Providers must mark AI-generated content so it is machine-readable and detectable, with a phase-in deadline of 2 December 2026 for systems already on the market.

How do I label my content as AI-generated?

Put a visible notice on the content itself — not buried in a caption — at first exposure, and add machine-readable provenance such as C2PA Content Credentials or a watermark. Keep a dated record of every labelled asset.

Does the EU AI Act apply to companies outside the EU?

Yes, it can. The Act applies to providers and deployers placing AI systems on the EU market or whose AI outputs are used in the EU, wherever the company is based. If EU users can access your AI content, check your obligations with qualified counsel.
