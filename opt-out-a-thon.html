FAQ

How long does a data broker opt-out take?

Most confirm within 72 hours and fully process within 2 to 8 weeks, depending on the broker. That's Them typically confirms within 72 hours. The listing stays visible while the request works through the queue, so re-check after a few days.

Do opt-outs last forever?

No. Brokers continuously ingest new public records and can quietly re-list you months later. Re-search your name on each broker every 3 to 6 months and re-submit when a listing reappears. Opting out is maintenance, not a one-time event.

Should I pay a data broker to remove my information?

No — legitimate opt-outs are always free. Never pay an individual broker a "removal fee." Paid services like DeleteMe or Kanary exist to do the repetitive re-submission work across dozens of brokers for you, which is a different thing from paying the broker itself.

Why aren't Radaris or PeekYou on the list?

Both lost their domains to Atlas Data Privacy Corporation under New Jersey's Daniel's Law — Radaris.com was seized by a Superior Court order on August 27, 2026, and PeekYou's domain was taken over after a settled lawsuit. Dead brokers need no opt-out.

Which data brokers should I remove myself from first?

People-search sites first: Whitepages and the PeopleConnect family (Intelius, TruthFinder, InstantCheckmate, USSearch). These are the listings most likely to be used for doxxing, stalking, and harassment — and their opt-out flows are the easiest on this page.
