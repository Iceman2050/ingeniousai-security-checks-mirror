    How the scoring works

    Each red flag carries a weight. Impersonation of a government agency (+25), legal threats like "arrest warrant" (+20), payment demands via gift cards or crypto (+20), urgency and secrecy tactics (+12 each), suspicious look-alike links (+15–20), and requests for your SSN or bank details (+12) stack up fast — and dangerous combinations (agency name plus a payment demand) add bonus points, because that's the classic scam shape.

    Verdict thresholds: 70–100 = High risk — likely a scam · 35–69 = Caution · 0–34 = Low risk.

    This is a heuristic screen, not proof. It looks for known scam patterns — it cannot verify who really sent a message, and a clever new scam may score low. The rules that never change: legitimate agencies never demand gift cards, crypto, or wire transfers; never use contact details from the suspicious message itself; when in doubt, hang up and verify independently.
