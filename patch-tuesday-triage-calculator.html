Why patch in this order

  • Exploited in the wild beats everything. CVE-2026-96951 is being attacked now, and it defeats Mark-of-the-Web — the exact defense that normally warns users about downloaded files. Per Microsoft's advisory, the attacker still needs the user to open the file, so pair the patch with a heads-up to users: treat unexpected attachments as hostile.
  • Publicly disclosed = exploit window open. CVE-2026-108398 and CVE-2026-92707 were public knowledge before fixes shipped, so every attacker and researcher can study them. They rank above "merely critical" items nobody knows about.
  • Then severity — matched to your stack. The Outlook preview-pane cluster needs no click beyond scrolling, which makes it nastier in practice than a critical flaw in a component you never installed. The calculator filters ~70 critical / 677 EoP / 317 RCE down to what you actually run.
  • Volume is not priority. 1,413 is the least useful number in the release — fewer than 1% of Microsoft's 2026 CVEs are known to be exploited. Triage by exploitability, not by count.

Key facts: October 2026 Patch Tuesday

  • Scale: a record 1,413 CVEs across 176 updates, released October 13, 2026 (per Microsoft's tally, reported by SecurityWeek) — roughly 1,102 in Windows, 134 in Office, 61 in developer tools, 36 in SharePoint Server.

Frequently asked questions

What was in the October 2026 Patch Tuesday?

Microsoft's October 2026 Patch Tuesday, released October 13, 2026, fixed a record 1,413 CVEs across 176 updates. The breakdown includes 1,102 flaws in Windows, 134 in Office, 61 in developer tools, 36 in SharePoint Server, plus Exchange Server, Azure, and Defender. Roughly 70 were rated critical, 677 were elevation-of-privilege, and 317 were remote code execution.

Which October 2026 Patch Tuesday vulnerability is exploited in the wild?

CVE-2026-96951, a Windows Shell security-feature bypass rated CVSS 8.8. It lets a specially crafted file evade Mark-of-the-Web (MotW) protections so it launches without the usual warnings when a user opens it. CISA added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on October 13, 2026, with a federal remediation deadline of October 16, 2026.

What day is Patch Tuesday this month and how often does it happen?

Patch Tuesday is the second Tuesday of every month. The October 2026 Patch Tuesday fell on October 13, 2026. Microsoft also ships out-of-band updates for actively exploited flaws between Patch Tuesdays.

What should I patch first after a huge Patch Tuesday?

A widely used triage order is: 1) vulnerabilities confirmed exploited in the wild (patch immediately), 2) vulnerabilities publicly disclosed before a fix was available, 3) remaining critical remote-code-execution and elevation-of-privilege flaws, filtered by what software you actually run. Always test patches on a small group before mass deployment.
