Frequently asked questions

Can this checklist detect the specific implant behind the $93M Ledger theft?

No. Public reporting is limited to Help Net Security's week-in-review noting a hidden implant linked to a $93M theft; no technical details of the implant have been published. This checklist covers verification hygiene that catches most supply-chain tampering — it is explicitly not a detector of that specific implant.

Where should I buy a hardware wallet to avoid a tampered device?

Buy only from the manufacturer's official store or an authorized retailer. Marketplace and reseller listings are the classic route for resealed or implanted devices.

My device came with a pre-filled recovery phrase. Is that safe?

No. A recovery phrase must be generated by your device, by you, during setup. A pre-printed or pre-filled phrase means someone else can already derive your keys. Do not use the device — generate a fresh wallet or buy a new device from a trusted source.

How do I confirm a Ledger device is genuine?

Use Ledger Live's built-in genuine check, which attests the device's secure element; install firmware only through Ledger Live; and always verify the receiving address shown on the device screen matches the one in Ledger Live before sending funds.
