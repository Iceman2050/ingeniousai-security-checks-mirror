How impersonation tricks work

Lookalike characters — "paypa1.com" swaps a letter for a number; "rnicrosoft.com" uses r+n to mimic m. Punycode — domains starting with xn-- can render as foreign characters that look identical to Latin letters. Hyphen stuffing — "secure-login-paypal-support.com" buries the real brand in noise. Abused TLDs — some cheap top-level domains show up in phishing far more often than others.
