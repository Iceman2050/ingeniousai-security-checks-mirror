One honest rule before you download anything

  • Only test what you own. Every tool above is legal to own and learn — running them against someone else's systems without written permission is illegal in most jurisdictions. Build a home lab or use intentionally vulnerable targets.
  • Tools don't pentest — people do. Scanners find known issues; they miss business-logic flaws, chained attacks, and anything novel. The tool is the starting kit, not the engagement.
  • Check the license. "Open source" covers many licenses (GPL, MIT, Apache and others) — check each repo before commercial use.
