What is shadow AI?

Shadow AI is any AI tool employees use without IT approval — personal ChatGPT accounts, free Copilot tiers, side Claude or Gemini usage. It becomes a security risk the moment company data flows into tools nobody monitors.

No judgment here. Shadow AI happens because the sanctioned tools are too slow, too locked-down, or don't exist. This page doesn't report you, shame you, or phone your CISO. It just grades the risk.

    Why is shadow AI a cybersecurity news emergency?

    Because it is the fastest-growing unmonitored data flow in most companies. Employees paste contracts, code, and customer data into unapproved AI tools daily — each one a breach vector with no logging, no DLP, and no deletion guarantee. Amnesty works better than prohibition: declare the tools, grade the risk honestly, then approve safe paths. You cannot secure what you pretend is not there.
