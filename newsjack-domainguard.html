FAQ

What is typosquatting?

Typosquatting is registering domain names that look like a trusted brand — swapped letters, added hyphens, lookalike Unicode characters, or a different TLD — to trick people into visiting a phishing site or trusting a scam email. The 30-second check hunts for them in certificate transparency logs.

How do I protect my brand from typosquatting?

Register the obvious lookalike variants of your brand yourself, repeat the certificate-transparency search regularly — squatters register new domains constantly — and consider continuous monitoring that alerts you the moment a lookalike is registered.

What is a typosquatted domain?

A typosquatted domain is a lookalike domain registered to exploit typing mistakes or visual similarity with a real brand. Attackers use them for phishing, credential theft, and brand impersonation — every one leaves a public trace in certificate transparency logs.
