What are ai agents for ads, and how do they work here?

Posting product promos every day is a treadmill: shoot, edit, resize for three platforms, write captions, post, repeat. Most small brands give up on consistency — and consistency is the whole game. The AI Promo Pipeline turns that treadmill into a system:

1. Product sheet in. You hand over your catalog — models, names, key details, prices. One input, no re-typing.
2. Claude crafts the ad copy. Hooks, captions, and calls-to-action written for each platform's style, not one caption copy-pasted three times.
3. AI builds the video and images. 15–60 second short-form videos optimized separately for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and Facebook — plus matching lifestyle product images generated on the fly.
4. Everything files itself. The finished package lands in your Google Drive, organized and named.
5. You approve on WhatsApp. Each draft package pauses and comes to your phone. You review, you tap yes — and only then does it schedule or publish.
6. Dashboard keeps score. Job status, retries, and error logs in one view, so you always know what ran, what posted, and what failed.

Why the approval gate is the headline

Fully autonomous posting is how brands end up apologizing on social media. The approval-gate pattern — a human says go before anything public happens — is what serious buyers are actually asking for: the same architecture was commissioned on the n8n jobs board by a buyer building an AI marketing content engine with a human approval gate. Automation for the grind, a human for the judgment. That's the whole design philosophy.
