What are AI video prompts for restaurants?

They are copy-and-paste instructions written for AI video generators. Each prompt specifies the shot, lighting, action, on-screen text, audio and duration, with brackets like [DISH NAME] and [RESTAURANT NAME] for your menu details. Paste one into a video tool, generate, and post the clip.

A good prompt does the director's work in advance: it fixes the shot, pace and mood before the tool renders a frame. Reuse it for every new dish by changing only the bracketed details.

Prompt #1 — The Sizzle Shot

Vertical 9:16 food commercial, 8 seconds. Extreme close-up of a thick [CUT OF MEAT] steak searing on a flaming charcoal grill, fat rendering and juices bubbling on the crust. Camera: slow push-in from 45-degree angle, shallow depth of field, glowing embers blurred in background. Lighting: warm firelight with dramatic orange highlights, dark moody background. Action: chef's tongs flip the steak at second 4, sparks fly, sizzle intensifies. On-screen text at end: "[DISH NAME] — [RESTAURANT NAME]". Audio: loud natural sizzle, crackling fire, no music. Photorealistic, ultra-detailed meat texture.

Prompt #2 — The Cheese Pull

Vertical 9:16 food video, 6 seconds. Close-up of a slice of [PIZZA TYPE] pizza being lifted from a whole pie, long strands of melted mozzarella stretching dramatically. Camera: static macro shot, slight slow-motion feel, cheese strands glistening. Lighting: bright warm overhead light, steam rising softly. Action: slice lifts slowly upward, cheese stretches to maximum length at second 4, then a clean break. On-screen text: "The pull says it all. [RESTAURANT NAME]". Audio: subtle stretchy cheese sound, soft ambient restaurant hum, no music. Photorealistic, rich golden cheese tones.

Prompt #3 — The Perfect Pour

Vertical 9:16 beverage video, 7 seconds. Close-up of [DRINK] being poured from a [VESSEL] into a clear glass — liquid cascading in a smooth unbroken stream, [FOAM/CONDENSATION DETAIL]. Camera: side angle at glass level, slow push-in as the pour completes. Lighting: bright natural window light, backlit so the liquid glows. Action: pour starts at second 1, glass fills to the brim by second 5, a final swirl at second 6. On-screen text: "[DRINK NAME] — poured fresh daily". Audio: gentle liquid pouring sound, soft cafe ambience. Photorealistic, crisp condensation droplets.
