Why do AI-referred visitors convert so much better?

Because the agent did the shopping before the visit. It compared options, read the terms, checked the price against the budget and eliminated your competitors. The visit that reaches your store is the end of a decision, not the start of one. That is why the traffic converts, and why a mechanical failure at your checkout wastes the most decided buyer you ever get.

Score your store's agent readiness

Answer as the agent sees your store: data first, design never. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Get agent-ready: the working order

  1. Feed the shortlist: complete, current product data: prices, stock, variants, in formats agents actually read.
  2. Move terms into data: shipping, returns and warranty as plain text in product data, not in banner images.
  3. Complete the path: walk your checkout as a machine and remove or repair every step an agent cannot finish.
  4. Measure the channel: separate agent traffic and completions in reporting, so readiness decay is visible.
  5. Retest on change: themes, apps and feed changes break agent paths silently. Agent-Ready Store Audit reruns the purchase path and renews the badge.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI shopping agent?

An AI shopping agent is an AI system that researches, compares and increasingly purchases products on a person's behalf. It reads stores as data: prices, variants, stock and terms. Agent-Ready Store Audit tests whether such an agent can complete a purchase on your store, and returns the fix list when it cannot.

What are AI agents doing to retail traffic?

Adobe Analytics measured AI-referred traffic to US retail sites up 393% in early 2026, with visitors converting about 42% better than non-AI traffic by March 2026 and roughly 60% better by July 2026. Since October 13, 2026, Shopify's Shop MCP also lets agents complete checkouts directly. The channel is small, growing fast and unusually valuable per visit.

What does agent-ready mean for a store?

Agent-ready means a machine buyer can do three things on your store: read the catalog as data to build a shortlist, credit your terms because they are plain text in the data, and complete the purchase path without a human-only step. Each is testable, and the audit tests them in that order.

How is this different from normal conversion optimization?

Conversion optimization persuades humans: design, copy, imagery, social proof. Agents are immune to all of it and allergic to missing data. Agent readiness is a data and path discipline, not a design one, which is why beautiful stores fail it and plain, well-structured stores pass.

How often should a store retest agent readiness?

Retest whenever the storefront changes: theme updates, new apps, feed changes, checkout changes. Agent paths break silently because no customer complains; the agent simply buys elsewhere. A standing retest keeps the pass badge honest and catches decay while it is still cheap.
