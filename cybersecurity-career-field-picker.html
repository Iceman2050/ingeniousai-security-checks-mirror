FAQ

How many fields are there in cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is not one job. It spans at least 26 distinct fields — protecting digital systems, risk management, network engineering, Linux, AI/ML security, incident response, cloud security, SOC operations, digital forensics, network security, malware analysis, ethical hacking, threat intelligence, penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, social engineering, Linux systems, scripting, OSINT, exploit development, wireless security, information security, security architecture, web application security, reverse engineering, and bug bounty.

How do I choose a cybersecurity career field?

Match the field to what you actually enjoy: breaking things legally (ethical hacking, penetration testing, bug bounty), deep analysis (malware analysis, reverse engineering, digital forensics), fast-paced defense (SOC operations, incident response), or strategy and design (risk management, security architecture). The free picker quiz above scores your interests against all 26 fields and recommends your top 3.

Do I need permission to practice hacking skills?

Yes — always. All cybersecurity activities must be performed legally, responsibly, and only with proper authorization. Practice on your own systems, in sanctioned lab environments (such as Hack The Box, TryHackMe, or CTF platforms), or in authorized bug bounty programs. Never test or access systems without permission.
