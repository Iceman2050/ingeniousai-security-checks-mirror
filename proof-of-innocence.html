Proof of Innocence

When an AI agent acts on your behalf — calling tools, moving money, sending messages — how do you prove what it did? Seal every action into a hash chain: each entry's fingerprint includes the fingerprint before it. Change one byte of history, and the whole chain breaks. Try it below.

Try it yourself
  1. Click Simulate agent actions to append 5 realistic log entries.
  2. Click Tamper with entry #2 — we quietly rewrite that entry's action text (like an attacker covering their tracks).
  3. Click Verify chain — every hash is recomputed. Watch it catch the forgery.
Why a hash chain works

Each entry stores hash = SHA-256(prev_hash + timestamp + actor + action). The next entry embeds that hash as its prev_hash, so entries are cryptographically linked in order. To forge entry #2 without detection, an attacker would have to recompute every later hash — and in the real product, hashes are also anchored server-side, so even that wouldn't survive.
