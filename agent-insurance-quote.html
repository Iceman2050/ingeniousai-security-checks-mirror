    Frequently asked questions

    Is this a real insurance quote?

    No. This is an educational mock policy document — a deterministic risk score and an illustrative premium band from your 5 answers. It is not an insurance offer and carries no underwriting.

    What are the security risks of AI agents?

    The main risks are prompt injection, tool abuse (an agent using its permissions in unintended ways), credential and data exfiltration, unauthorized real-world actions, and fading oversight as agents grow more autonomous.

    How do you stop an AI agent from going rogue?

    Use least-privilege permissions, require human approval for irreversible actions, enforce egress allowlists at the network layer rather than in the prompt, keep tamper-evident logs of every agent action, and maintain a working kill switch.

    How is the premium band calculated?

    A transparent, deterministic scoring model turns your five answers into a risk grade (A–F) and an illustrative band. It is reproducible, not actuarial — useful for thinking about risk, not for buying coverage.
