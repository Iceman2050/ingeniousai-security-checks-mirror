What changed in AI terms of service most recently?

In the October 17, 2026 check, Anthropic announced a usage-policy overhaul effective November 12, 2026, adding conduct rules for users, and Meta removed the word "train" from its commercial terms while continuing to crawl business sites for ads and business products. OpenAI, Google, and xAI showed no verifiable change that week. Dates and sources are on the timeline above.

Does ChatGPT train on my data?

It depends on your plan's settings — and terms change, which is why the timeline exists. In the October 17, 2026 check, OpenAI had no verifiable change: its usage-policy changelog tops out at October 29, 2025. Always verify against the current policy.

What did Meta's terms take from businesses?

The original commercial-terms clause took business-linked site content to "train" AI products with no opt-out. After October 17, 2026, the word "train" is gone — but broad crawling for ads and business products remains, and Meta says its crawlers operate as before.
