Put a hard cap on the meter

  • Set a daily token budget with a kill switch. Per-token billing means an uncapped agent has an uncapped bill. A budget ceiling turns a runaway thread from a $1,900/month surprise into a stopped process.
  • Cap turns per conversation. A maximum-turns rule (with a graceful hand-off to a human) kills loops structurally, not by hope.
  • Truncate re-sent history. Long threads re-send their whole history every turn — summarization or a sliding context window cuts the compounding term in this calculator.
  • Alert on per-thread spend, not monthly totals. By the time a monthly report shows the spike, the money is gone. Per-thread thresholds catch it the same day.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Meta charge for WhatsApp Business Agent tokens?

Per published reporting, Meta bills WhatsApp Business Agent usage at $2 per 1 million tokens, in effect since August 1, 2026. That is a metered AI charge on top of WhatsApp's regular per-conversation messaging pricing — every token your agent reads or generates is billable.

How many tokens does one WhatsApp AI message use?

Published estimates put a single AI agent message at roughly 20,000–25,000 tokens once you count the system prompt, conversation history, tool definitions, and retrieved context that ride along with every reply — not just the visible words. This calculator defaults to 22,500 tokens per message, the midpoint of that cited range, and lets you change it.

What does one WhatsApp AI conversation cost at $2 per 1M tokens?

At the default 22,500 tokens per message and $2 per 1M tokens, one AI message costs about $0.045. At 100 AI messages a day that is $4.50/day, or about $135/month — before long threads. Long, looping conversations are where the bill stops being linear, which is why this calculator models them separately.

What is a runaway thread and why does it matter?

A runaway thread is a single conversation that keeps generating — an agent stuck in a retry loop, two bots talking to each other, or a customer who keeps a thread alive for hours. Because billing is per token with no natural stopping point, one looping conversation generating a full AI response every minute burns about 32.4 million tokens a day at the default message size: roughly $64.80/day from a single thread.

How do I track my real WhatsApp agent token usage instead of estimating?

Export your agent's run logs and total the prompt and completion tokens per conversation, then multiply by your rate per million. The companion hatch-token-meter CLI on this page does exactly that from a log file, so you can compare the real bill against this estimate. For live protection, put a hard budget cap and approval queue in front of the agent so a runaway thread is stopped by a limit, not discovered on an invoice.
