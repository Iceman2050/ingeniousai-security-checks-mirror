The AI Vendor Dead-Pool: AI Startup Shutdown Signals to Watch

Every year, AI vendors vanish — sometimes with a bankruptcy filing, sometimes with a quiet sunset email, sometimes by burying the product inside a buyer. This page tracks the public signals, dated and sourced, so you can see which of your AI dependencies are wobbling before they fall.

⚠ This is not a prediction of closure

Nothing here predicts that any specific company will die. Every entry is a "signal to watch" — a dated, publicly sourced fact such as an announced shutdown, a bankruptcy filing, or a model retirement. Model retirements are labeled as such: a vendor retiring old models is not the same thing as the vendor dying. If a claim cannot be checked against a public source, it does not appear on this page.

Character.ai — Chapter 11 bankruptcy, sale by early December

Financial distress
Signal strength (editorial read — not a prediction): 5/5
Strongest signal on this page: the company itself says the platform will be wound down if no buyer keeps it running.

Character Technologies, Inc. filed a voluntary Chapter 11 petition on October 12, 2026, in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware, listing liabilities of $50–100 million. Disney is the stalking-horse bidder at $20 million for certain assets plus a non-exclusive technology license — a bid for the engine, not for running the platform as-is. The company says the platform stays up during a court-supervised sale expected to close in late November or early December 2026, and warns that "the platform will be wound down" if no bid allows continued operation. CEO Colin Adams cited "significant legal, regulatory, and operational headwinds," including lawsuits over chatbots' effects on teenagers. Google is the largest listed creditor at $4.15 million.

Mozilla Solo — AI website builder fully deleted Nov 30

Announced shutdown
Signal strength (editorial read — not a prediction): 5/5
Hard deletion date announced by the vendor itself; no recovery possible after.

Mozilla announced it is closing Solo, the free AI website builder it launched in late 2023. New signups are already closed. Every Solo website, account and file is deleted on November 30, 2026, with no way to recover anything afterward. Active Pro and Grow subscribers receive a prorated refund for unused time calculated from October 1, 2026. Image source files are not included in the data export — only links, which die with Solo.

Tencent QClaw — desktop AI assistant shut Dec 24

Announced shutdown
Signal strength (editorial read — not a prediction): 5/5
Permanent shutdown date announced by Tencent; staff already moved to another product.

Tencent announced on September 24, 2026, that its desktop AI assistant QClaw would immediately cease new user registrations ahead of a permanent shutdown on December 24 — less than ten months after its March 2026 public launch. The QClaw team was folded into the business unit managing WorkBuddy, Tencent's AI workspace platform. NetEase Youdao launched a migration campaign for QClaw users the same month.

OpenAI's Oct 23, 2026 model retirement wave

Model retirement
Signal strength (editorial read — not a prediction): 3/5
This is a vendor retiring models, not a vendor dying — but it is exactly how AI dependencies break silently.

On October 23, 2026, OpenAI retires a large batch of models per its published deprecations calendar: gpt-4-0613, gpt-4-turbo, gpt-4-1106-preview, o1, o1-pro, o3-mini, o4-mini, gpt-image-1, and others — moved to the gpt-5.6 family. It follows earlier 2026 retirements including the Assistants API (Aug 26), Sora 2 (Sep 24), and DALL·E 3 (May 12). Any app still pinned to these model names breaks on the date unless migrated.

ChatGPT Atlas — OpenAI's browser shut down Aug 9

Product retirement
Signal strength (editorial read — not a prediction): 2/5
Healthy vendor pruning a failed product — normal portfolio churn, but a reminder that products die faster than companies.

OpenAI shut down ChatGPT Atlas — its Chromium-based browser launched October 21, 2025 — on August 9, 2026, 292 days after launch. The browser never left macOS: promised Windows, iOS and Android builds never shipped even as public betas. OpenAI folded the work into a ChatGPT Chrome extension and a browser mode in the desktop app rather than keep a standalone browser alive.

Why we run a dead-pool

The AI tools that power a business can die three ways: the company folds (Character.ai), the vendor announces a sunset (Mozilla Solo, Tencent QClaw), or the vendor silently retires the model your app is pinned to (OpenAI's Oct 23 wave). The tool dying is survivable. The data dying with it — or your product breaking on a retirement date nobody flagged — is not. That is the entire point of a model deprecation radar.

Frequently asked questions

Is Character.AI shutting down?

Not immediately. Character Technologies, Inc. filed a voluntary Chapter 11 bankruptcy petition on October 12, 2026, in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware. The platform remains up and running during a court-supervised sale expected to close in late November or early December 2026. If no bid emerges that allows continued operation, the platform will be wound down.

What is an AI startup shutdown signal?

A shutdown signal is a public, checkable fact that a vendor may be in trouble: a bankruptcy filing, an announced shutdown date, a product retirement, a model deprecation wave, or reported financial distress. This page tracks signals, not predictions — every entry cites a dated source, and model retirements are labeled as such rather than company deaths.

How can I tell if an AI tool my business uses is about to shut down?

Watch for the signals tracked here: bankruptcy filings, announced shutdown or deletion dates, model retirement calendars, asset sales, and discontinued signup pages. The practical defenses are always the same: export your data on demand, own your domain in your own registrar account, avoid storing anything you cannot afford to lose, and run a model deprecation radar over your AI dependencies.

What happens to my data when an AI tool shuts down?

It depends on the vendor. Mozilla's own shutdown FAQ for Solo says every website, account and file is deleted on November 30, 2026, with no way to recover anything afterward. Some vendors offer exports and prorated refunds; others simply go dark. Check the vendor's shutdown notice, export before the deadline, and treat "no grace period" as the default assumption.
