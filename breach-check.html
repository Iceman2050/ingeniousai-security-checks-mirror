Have my credentials leaked?

Here is the truth most "breach checker" sites will not tell you: a static web page cannot safely check breach databases for you. Any site that asks for your email and instantly invents a verdict is either harvesting your address or making the answer up. Here is the honest 5-minute check instead.

The 3 real steps

1

Check Have I Been Pwned yourself

Run by security researcher Troy Hunt. It uses k-anonymity, so your full email never leaves your browser during the lookup. Type your address there — not here.

Open Have I Been Pwned →
2

Kill password reuse with a password manager + passkeys

Breaches hurt because one leaked password unlocks ten accounts. A password manager gives every site a unique password; passkeys remove passwords entirely where supported. Do this for email first — your inbox is the master key to everything else.

3

If you were in a breach: contain it today

Change the password everywhere you reused it, turn on two-factor authentication on every important account, and expect phishing emails that reference the breach — attackers buy these lists and personalize their lures.
