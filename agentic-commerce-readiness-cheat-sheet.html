What does agentic commerce readiness check?

Readiness checks whether each step of the buying path works for a non-human buyer. Can an agent discover the product and read its true price and options? Can it add to cart and complete checkout without hitting a step only a human with a mouse can pass? And does confirmation arrive in a form both the agent and the customer can verify afterwards?

  • Discoverability: products findable and identifiable by an agent — stable URLs, clear titles, structured data.
  • Price readability: the real price, variants, taxes and shipping readable as data, not only as styled page decoration.
  • Cart and checkout completion: the full path completable by an agent, with no invisible blockers or human-only traps mid-flow.
  • Policy clarity: shipping, returns and terms reachable and readable before purchase, where an agent (and its owner) looks for them.
  • Confirmation and records: an order confirmation that states what was bought, for how much, and how to undo or return it.

What does the simulated purchase path look like?

  1. Arrive as an agent would — from a search result or a direct product link, with no insider knowledge of your store's layout.
  2. Select a real product and variant, reading price and options from the page as data.
  3. Complete the purchase path up to the payment boundary, noting every step that blocks, misleads or silently changes the price.
  4. Verify the confirmation — order details, totals and next steps legible to a machine and a human.
  5. Score and list fixes in the order a buyer hits them, so the team fixes the path in sequence.

Why does this matter now?

Agent buyers do not improvise around a broken step the way a motivated human does — they abandon the path and buy from the store whose path worked. Readiness is therefore a revenue property: every blocker on the simulated path is a sale an agent customer will complete elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

What is agentic commerce?

Agentic commerce is buying done by AI agents on behalf of people: the agent finds the product, reads the price and terms, completes checkout and reports back. Stores that agents can successfully buy from get those sales; stores with agent-blocking steps in the path silently lose them.

What is agentic commerce readiness?

Readiness is whether an AI agent can complete your store's whole purchase path — discovery, product and price reading, cart, checkout and confirmation — without human-only blockers. Store Audit measures it with a simulated purchase and returns a plain-language fix list for every step that fails.

Why would an agent fail to buy from my store?

Common blockers are prices that only exist as styled images, variant selectors that need precise mouse interaction, checkout steps that change totals without explanation, and policy pages an agent cannot find. Humans work around these; agents abandon the path and buy where the path works.
