Frequently asked questions

What was the biggest data breach of 2017?

Equifax, disclosed in September 2017, exposed the personal data of about 147 million people and led to a settlement of up to $700 million announced by the FTC in July 2019.

How much did the Target 2013 breach cost?

Target reported approximately $292 million in breach-related costs, per its public disclosures. Adjusted for inflation, that is roughly $411 million in 2026 dollars.

Which breach had the most victims ever?

Yahoo's 2013 breach, disclosed in 2016 and later revised, affected all 3 billion user accounts — the largest publicly disclosed breach by victim count.

Are the cost figures on this page verified?

Yes — cost figures appear only where a real figure was publicly reported by the company or a regulator, and each breach links to published reporting. Where no cost was disclosed, the page says so explicitly.
