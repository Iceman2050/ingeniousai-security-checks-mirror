Frequently asked questions

Is this based on a real incident?

No. Every stage is labeled SIMULATION. The scenario is fictional and designed as a tabletop-style exercise; no vendor is named and no real incident is referenced.

What does the simulation teach?

Incident-response discipline: contain first, preserve evidence, verify scope before notifying, communicate honestly under pressure, and fix the logging gap in the retrospective.

How long does it take?

Four stages, each with a generous two-minute countdown you can skip. Most players finish in 5 to 10 minutes.

What is Agent Flight Recorder?

An Ingenious AI platform that keeps tamper-evident, timestamped logs of every AI agent action — the kind of evidence trail the simulation's retrospective recommends. Early access is available via WhatsApp.
