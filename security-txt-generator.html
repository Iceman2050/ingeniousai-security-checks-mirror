Frequently asked questions

What is security.txt?

security.txt is a plain text file defined by RFC 9116, published in April 2022. Placed at /.well-known/security.txt, it tells security researchers exactly how to report vulnerabilities they find in your systems — which contact to use, whether encrypted reports are accepted, and where your disclosure policy lives.

Which fields does RFC 9116 require?

Two fields are required: Contact (at least one mailto:, https: or tel: URI) and Expires (an ISO 8601 date-time, recommended less than a year ahead so the file cannot go stale). Everything else — Encryption, Acknowledgments, Policy, Hiring, Preferred-Languages, Canonical — is optional but useful.

Where do I put the generated file?

Upload it to https://yourdomain.com/.well-known/security.txt and make sure it is served as plain text over HTTPS. Most static hosts, CDNs and CMS platforms let you add a file at that path in minutes.

Does my data leave my browser?

No. This generator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere — the file is assembled locally and downloaded straight to your device.
