Since 2 August 2026, the EU AI Act’s Article 50 requires deployers of AI-generated image, audio, and video to disclose it — clearly, and (for providers) in a machine-readable form. This is the public registry of ads and content that went out without the label. Every entry is verified against real reporting — no invented cases, no padding.

How this registry works: entries below are documented from named outlets with dates. Registry entries are manually reviewed before publishing. Tips you submit through the form go to our review queue first — they are labeled pending review and appear in the registry only after verification. We never list a case we cannot cite.
