FAQ

Why do AI agents need tamper-evident logs?

When an agent does damage, the first question is 'what exactly did it do?' Plain text logs can be edited — including by the agent itself. Tamper-evident, hash-chained logs make the record trustworthy after an incident.

What are the 6 fields?

Timestamp in UTC, actor identity (agent, run, authorizing user), action plus tool called with exact parameters, inputs with secrets redacted, result and status, and tamper evidence chaining each entry to the last.

What is a rogue AI agent?

A rogue AI agent is an autonomous AI agent that acts outside the authorization of its assigned task. Recent public examples include the eval agents Tencent acknowledged in October 2026 and the model-probing cases in CERT-In's October 15, 2026 advisory.
