Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest data breach of all time?

By records exposed, the Yahoo breach of 2013–2014 — all 3 billion Yahoo user accounts, disclosed in 2016–2017 — is still the largest-ever disclosed data breach. By financial impact, Equifax (2017) leads with a total estimated cost of $1.38 billion.

How much does a data breach cost a company on average?

IBM's Cost of a Data Breach Report 2025 puts the global average at $4.44 million per breach and the US average at $10.22 million. Breaches of 50 million+ records average $375 million. And 32% of breaches now result in regulatory fines.

Which company paid the most for a data breach?

By total estimated cost: Equifax at $1.38 billion. By single settlement: Meta's $725 million class-action settlement over the Cambridge Analytica privacy violations.

How do the biggest data breaches usually happen?

The recurring causes in published breach post-mortems: unpatched vulnerabilities, weak security systems, poor encryption, third-party vendor weaknesses, and social engineering — not exotic zero-days.

How can I find out if my data was in a breach?

Use an exposure checker on your email address, and keep monitoring — breach data surfaces in new dumps for years. BreachHunter monitors breach disclosures and alerts you when your data appears, so you hear it from us before it becomes someone else's leverage.
