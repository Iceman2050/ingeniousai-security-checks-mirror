The pain: paper manifests run the operation, and they run it badly

A parcel depot, a delivery fleet, a construction site: the field speaks paper. Manifests, delivery dockets, timesheets, inspection checklists, inventory counts. At the end of the shift those sheets travel — in a pocket, in a clipboard, in a van — and eventually someone re-types them into a spreadsheet. That gap is where the errors live: skipped lines, transposed quantities, illegible handwriting, sheets that never arrive at all. One wrong quantity on a manifest becomes a wrong invoice, a wrong stock count, a dispute you can't win because the paper is gone.

The usual answers don't fit field staff. Scanners need hardware and a desk. Dedicated data-entry apps need training and logins your couriers won't keep. The field already has one tool everybody knows: WhatsApp. The field-to-sheet pipeline meets them there — photograph, send, done — and moves the discipline to the system, not the person.

How it works: the 5-step flow

1. Photo. A courier or field worker photographs the manifest, docket, or form and sends it to your WhatsApp intake number. No new app, no training.
2. Extract. The n8n workflow picks up the photo and sends it to AI vision extraction — line items, quantities, and reference numbers become structured fields.
3. Stage. Rows land in a staging Google Sheet with confidence flags. Your live operations sheet is untouched — staging is where imperfect reads wait.
4. Approve. The supervisor gets a WhatsApp batch summary and taps thumbs-up to release the rows — or replies with a correction that gets applied first.
5. Live. Approved rows (corrections included) are written to the live operations sheet. Every step is logged with automatic retries on failure.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does the field-to-sheet pipeline do?

It turns paper-manifest photos into validated spreadsheet rows. Your couriers or field staff photograph manifests, delivery dockets, or field forms and send them to a WhatsApp intake number. An n8n workflow sends each photo to AI vision extraction, which reads the line items into a staging Google Sheet. Nothing reaches your live operations sheet until a supervisor reviews the batch on WhatsApp and taps approve — or replies with a correction. You get the workflow export, the Sheets template, a Loom walkthrough, and 1 week of support.

How does the WhatsApp approval gate work?

After extraction, the pipeline sends your supervisor a WhatsApp message summarizing the batch: manifest ID, number of rows, and any low-confidence fields. The supervisor taps thumbs-up (or a pre-agreed keyword) to release the rows to the live sheet, or replies with a correction like "row 3: qty 24 not 42". Corrections are applied before the rows go live, and every approval and correction is logged with a timestamp.

How accurate is the AI extraction?

We quote no guaranteed accuracy number — AI vision reads clean printed manifests well, and handwriting is where errors concentrate. That's exactly why the pipeline has a staging sheet and a human approval gate: extraction is the draft, the supervisor's tap is the accuracy mechanism. Low-confidence fields are flagged for review, and the correction loop is built in, not bolted on.
