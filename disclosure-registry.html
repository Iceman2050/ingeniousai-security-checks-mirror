FAQ — EU AI Act disclosure

Does AI-generated content have to be labelled under the EU AI Act?

Yes, in most cases. Deployers of AI-generated image, audio or video must disclose it as AI-generated — the Article 50(4) deployer disclosure duty has applied since 2 August 2026.

What are the labelling requirements for AI-generated content?

The notice must be clear and distinguishable, and given at first exposure. Providers must mark AI-generated content so it is machine-readable and detectable, with a phase-in deadline of 2 December 2026.

How do I label my content as AI-generated?

Put a visible notice on the content itself — not buried in a caption — at first exposure, and add machine-readable provenance such as C2PA Content Credentials or a watermark.

Does the EU AI Act apply to companies outside the EU?

Yes, it can. The Act applies to providers and deployers placing AI systems on the EU market or whose AI outputs are used in the EU, wherever the company is based.
