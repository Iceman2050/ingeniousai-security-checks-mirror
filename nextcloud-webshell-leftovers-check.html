Why patching isn't enough

  • Sept 11, 2026: fix released in richdocumentscode 26.4.303 — but the CVE advisory didn't land until Oct 14, and exploitation was already happening in September.
  • Reporting on the incident is explicit: an update does not remove a shell already planted, so patching and checking for persistence are separate tasks.
  • On compromised servers, attackers searched for credential stores and crypto wallets (KeePass databases, wallet.dat). No root access was reported, and reviewed requests showed discovery rather than confirmed exfiltration — but anything readable by the web-server account should be treated as exposed.
  • Reports of ransomware operators exploiting the flaw are unconfirmed (BleepingComputer).
