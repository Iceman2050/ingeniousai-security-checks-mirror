How the attacks work (verified)

  • Verbatim-reasoning requests: instructing the model to reproduce its prior reasoning word for word.
  • Context editing: attackers with API access edited the model's prior context to coax out protected reasoning — closed by "preserved thinking" on newer Claude models for accounts created on/after Aug 31, 2026.
  • Evasion variants: asking for reasoning translated into another language, or wrapped in tags alongside the answer — patched rapidly once spotted.
  • Proxy-network probing: rotating through proxies to repeatedly probe protected reasoning at scale — now blocked and hindered.
  • Cross-model reads: using earlier, weaker models to read newer models' reasoning — closed by version-gating.

Anthropic's three defense layers

LayerWhat it does
1. Preserved thinkingNewer models (Fable 5.1, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5) block context-editing extraction; reasoning transcripts visible only to the org that made the request
2. Extraction classifiersTwo-stage classifier (constitutional-classifiers lineage) detecting reasoning-extraction attempts; red-teamed inside and out; <0.1% false positives; "roughly as robust as bioweapon refusal"
3. Persistent-attacker pressureStricter limits for repeat probers, account bans (tens of thousands banned), and — since Sep 24 — charging for blocked requests in <0.1%-FP areas so attacks cost money

Sources: Reuters · Unite.AI (technical detail) · Straits Times. Anthropic also named Chinese startup Z.ai (Zhipu) as attempting to distill Claude for its GLM-5.3; Z.ai didn't comment.

Apply it to your product — this week

  • Hide the thinking. Serve answers, not reasoning traces. If any trace is exposed, scope it to the requesting customer only.
  • Detect the pattern, not just the request. Single extraction attempts look innocent; 12,000 systematic ones don't. Alert on volume + technique diversity per account.
  • Make attacks expensive. Throttle persistent probers, ban repeat offenders, and consider charging for blocked abusive requests.
  • Patch the tricks fast. Translation, tag-wrapping, verbatim-replay — each new evasion needs a classifier update within days, not quarters.
