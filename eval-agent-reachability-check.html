Why does an eval sandbox become a production risk?

Because evals reward realism. Teams copy production configs into the eval environment, hand the agent production-shaped credentials, and leave both in place after the run. The agent under test then holds a working path into real systems, and the first outsider to find that path inherits it.

Close the eval-to-production path: the order matters

  1. Map the route: list every way the eval environment can reach production: network paths, shared buckets, shared queues, shared credentials. If a path exists on paper, treat it as open.
  2. Rotate before you tidy: any real secret that has lived in an eval config is already exposed. Rotate it first; cleaning the file afterwards does not un-expose the old key.
  3. Scope the role to the eval: the agent under test needs the dataset and nothing else. Read-only, one environment, expiring with the run.
  4. Scan the declared files: run a reachability scan over the repo files and configs your eval actually loads, and fix findings by severity. This is the scan EvalScope automates.
  5. Keep evidence: keep the scan output with your EU AI Act documentation. When an auditor asks how you know the eval surface is contained, a dated scan beats a memory.

Frequently asked questions

What is eval-agent reachability?

Eval-agent reachability is the set of systems, data and actions an agent under evaluation can actually touch from inside the eval environment. It is defined by the eval's network routes, the secrets present in its configs and the permissions on its role. EvalScope measures it from your declared files, so the answer is evidence, not opinion.

What is CERT-In advisory CIAD-2026-0049?

CIAD-2026-0049 is an advisory from CERT-In, India's national incident response authority, published on October 15, 2026. It names three weakness classes in AI evaluation environments: exposed secrets in eval configs, permissive roles granted to eval agents, and open configuration surfaces. The advisory is the reason this check exists.

Does the EU AI Act cover evaluation setups?

The EU AI Act regulates AI systems by risk class and role, and its transparency obligations have applied since August 2, 2026. An eval environment that can reach production data undermines the governance story your documentation tells. Whether a specific duty applies to your setup is a legal call; the reachability facts underneath it are measurable.

How often should eval reachability be checked?

Check it whenever the eval environment changes: new datasets, new tools for the agent, new infrastructure, or a copied production config. Eval setups drift toward realism over time, and drift is exactly how production secrets and routes arrive. A scan per change keeps the evidence current for audits.
