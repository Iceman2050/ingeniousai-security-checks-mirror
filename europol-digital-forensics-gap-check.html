E-EVIDENCE — OCT 2026

Europol just admitted forensics can't keep pace with evidence.

On Oct 16, 2026, Europol published Digital Forensics: Gap Analysis — coordinated with the European Commission — finding that investigative tools, resources and expertise are struggling to keep pace with the volume and forms of digital evidence. Encryption, cloud sprawl, IoT devices and connected vehicles all complicate recovery; capabilities vary wildly across member states. But the flip side matters for you: if your org was breached or served a legal request tomorrow, could you produce evidence? Five questions.

Short answer: keep a year of logs, document chain of custody, make encryption recoverable, and know exactly where your cloud data lives. Evidence you can't produce is evidence that doesn't exist.

What the Europol report found (verified)

  • Tools can't keep pace: forensic tools, resources and expertise are struggling with the forms and volume of digital evidence modern tech creates.
  • Access is the bottleneck: encryption, cloud storage and custom-built technologies complicate lawful access; connected vehicles and IoT need specialist methods.
  • Uneven across the EU: capabilities vary across member states; tools are expensive and quickly outdated; differing data formats and validation practices hinder combining results across borders.
  • Known solutions unused: existing European forensic solutions aren't always widely known or used — a coordination problem, not just a tech one.
  • Your takeaway: if law enforcement struggles to extract evidence, organizations must make their own evidence producible — logging, preservation, and key management are on you.

Sources: Europol · European Commission.

Get investigation-ready — this month

  • Log everything, keep it a year. Centralized, tamper-evident security logs are the single highest-value forensic asset.
  • Write the chain-of-custody playbook now. Who images devices, with what tool, who signs for the copy — decided during calm, not crisis.
  • Make encryption recoverable. Full-disk encryption with escrowed keys: you stay protected from theft, and a lawful request doesn't hit a dead end.
  • Map your cloud data. Know which services hold what, and test that you can export it in a forensically usable format.
